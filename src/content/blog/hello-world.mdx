I'm a software engineer working on .NET backends, search and RAG systems ([more about me](/about/)).
My day job keeps me close to things like vector indexes, ranking and LLM agents, and I learn them best
by building them from scratch and watching them run.

So this site is my workshop: [projects](/projects/) like [Jarvis](/projects/jarvis/), [interactive
visualizations](/visualize/) of the algorithms and ML models I've learned, and posts like this one.

Posts are MDX, so they can include regular Markdown, code:

```ts
const hll = new HyperLogLog(14);
for (const id of visitorIds) hll.add(id);
console.log(`~${Math.round(hll.estimate())} unique visitors`);
```

…and interactive components, just like the visualization pages.
